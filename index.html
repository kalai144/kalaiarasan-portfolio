Surface Defect Detection on Weldments

AI based ANFIS algorithm system used to detect welding defects and reduce inspection time in manufacturing. Using the Adaptive Neuro-Fuzzy Inference System (ANFIS) algorithm involves combining the learning capabilities of Neural Networks with the human-like reasoning of Fuzzy Logic to identify, classify, and predict welding flaws. ANFIS is particularly effective for mapping nonlinear relationships between welding process parameters (like current, speed, and voltage) and output quality characteristics (like surface roughness or bead width).
